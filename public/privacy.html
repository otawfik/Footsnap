Privacy Policy

FootSnap · last updated 19 October 2026

FootSnap shows football fixtures, live scores, tables, scorers, squads and model-based predictions. It is built so that it doesn't need to know who you are.

What FootSnap collects

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites. The app doesn't access your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

Requests to FootSnap's server

To load football data, the app makes requests to FootSnap's server (hosted by Vercel), which fetches the data from football-data.org and caches it. As with any website, the hosting provider receives your IP address and basic request details (time, the page or data requested, and your device's user agent) in order to deliver the response, and keeps standard server logs for a limited period. FootSnap doesn't use these logs to identify or profile anyone. Your IP address isn't passed on to football-data.org.

Player photos

When you open a player card, the app requests that player's photo directly from Wikipedia. Wikipedia receives that request as it would from a browser; see the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy.

Links

Links to football-data.org, Wikipedia or GitHub open in your browser and are governed by those sites' own policies.

Children

FootSnap collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
