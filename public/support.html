FootSnap Support

Live scores, tables and predictions for Europe's top five leagues and the Champions League.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/otawfik/Footsnap/issues describing what you saw, which league and screen, and your device. Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

A score looks behind. Live scores refresh every 30 seconds while a match is on, and the free data feed can itself run a minute or two behind the broadcast. Pull down on any screen to refresh.

"Busy for a moment". The data provider limits how often it can be asked. The app retries on its own; wait a few seconds and pull to refresh.

Where do predictions come from? A Poisson goals model fitted to this season's results, refit after every result. The Predictions tab shows how often it has called results correctly this season. They're estimates, not betting advice.

A player photo is missing or wrong. Photos come from Wikipedia and are matched by name; if there's no clear match the card shows a silhouette instead. Report a wrong photo through the link above.

Privacy

FootSnap has no accounts and collects no personal data. Read the privacy policy.
